The registration pathway

START WITH JUNIOR.
AIM FOR TIMBERJACKS.

01

CHECK THE CURRENT CLL GUIDANCE

Review age eligibility, boundaries, fees and available registration periods on the official registration page. The published guidance currently describes the 2026 season.

02

REGISTER THROUGH RAMP

CLL directs Timberjacks applicants to the Junior Division. Use CLL’s RAMP portal to sign in or create your account.

03

FOLLOW THE EVALUATION PROCESS

Selected players pay an additional Timberjacks program fee after evaluations. Junior registration is not a guarantee of Timberjacks selection.
