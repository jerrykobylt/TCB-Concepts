How this works

We build first and talk after.

Nobody signs anything to see their concept. We would rather show you what your organization could have than describe it in a meeting.

1

We choose a project

Usually one we already noticed — a team, a non-profit, a shop whose work deserves more attention than its website gives it.

2

We build the concept

A real, working site in your colours with your content, at our cost. You get a private link you can forward to your board.

3

You tear it apart

Tell us what we got wrong, what is missing, and what your volunteers actually need. This is the part that matters most.

4

We finish it, or we don’t

Launch it with hosting, domain and updates handled. Or take the files and run it yourself. Walking away costs you nothing.

Who we say yes to

We choose the projects, and we say no often.

This is not an agency and we are not trying to fill a pipeline. A small team can only do a few of these properly at once, so we would rather be honest about how we pick.

  • You serve the Tri-Cities Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Coquitlam, Anmore or Belcarra. Local is the whole point.
  • The work is worth more attention than it gets Teams, non-profits, clubs, small businesses and creatives who already do the thing well.
  • Someone on your side can decide One person who can say yes. Projects that need six approvals stall and everybody loses.
  • You will actually use it A site nobody updates dies in a year. We want it kept current after we hand it over.
  • You are willing to be on the Board Your events, listings, volunteer roles and stories, kept current on Tri-Cities Board.

Why we are doing this at all

Tri-Cities Board is free to the community. Residents browse and join for nothing, and organizations, businesses and creatives get discovery and engagement without a bill.

That only works if the good ones are actually on it. So instead of selling ads at people, we build the thing they were never going to get around to, and ask them to show up on the Board in return.

It is a trade, not a favour. You get a website you would have paid thousands for. The Tri-Cities gets one more place worth finding.

What it costs

We would rather collaborate than invoice.

Both doors are open. Most organizations we work with come through the first one.

What we prefer

Collaboration

No invoice

We build, host and maintain the site. You bring your organization onto Tri-Cities Board and keep it alive there.

  • A full rebuild, designed and built by us
  • Hosting, domain help and ongoing updates
  • Your events, programs and volunteer roles posted on the Board
  • A claimed, verified profile in the local directory
  • Coverage on Boardwide Media when there is something to cover

Paid work

Quoted per project, local rates

Some organizations need to own it outright, on their own timeline, with a contract and a support agreement behind it. That is a fair thing to want.

  • Your timeline and your scope, in writing
  • Full ownership and handover of the files
  • A named support agreement instead of goodwill
  • No obligation to be on the Board
  • Quoted after we understand the job, never before

Ask us

Tell us what you are working with.

A few honest sentences beat a polished brief. If it is a fit, we will come back with questions and start building. If it is not, we will tell you that plainly and quickly.

  • No cost, no contract, and no sales call booked behind your back.
  • A real person reads every one of these. Usually within a week.
  • We will say no if we cannot do it justice, and we will say why.
  • Nothing you send here gets published without your say-so.
